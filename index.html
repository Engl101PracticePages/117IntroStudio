Who Are You?

Use this studio to plan and create your two-minute class introduction. You have two choices: record a video introduction, or create slides with a voiceover. Your final presentation should help classmates get to know you and explain how ENGL& 101 might benefit you in school, your career path, or your personal life.

Canvas Upload Walkthrough

Choose how you are posting your video. The walkthrough will show only those steps.

Canvas discussion reply
  1. Open the Canvas discussion and click Reply.
  2. Write a short greeting or context sentence.
  3. Use the attachment, file upload, or media upload option to add your video file.
  4. Wait for the upload to finish before posting.
  5. Post your reply, then play the video to make sure classmates can hear and see it.
Post check: Your reply should include a greeting plus a working video upload.
  1. Upload your video to Google Drive.
  2. Open sharing settings and choose Anyone with the link.
  3. Make sure the permission says Viewer.
  4. Copy the link and paste it into your Canvas discussion reply.
  5. Open the link from Canvas to make sure it works.
Post check: Your reply should include a greeting plus a Drive link classmates can view.

Canvas Upload Walkthrough

Choose how you are posting your slides with voiceover. The walkthrough will show only those steps.

Canvas discussion reply
  1. Open the Canvas discussion and click Reply.
  2. Write a short greeting or context sentence.
  3. Upload the exported slideshow video or voiceover presentation file.
  4. Wait for the upload to finish before posting.
  5. Post your reply, then play the file to make sure the voiceover can be heard.
Post check: Your reply should include a greeting plus a working file with voiceover.
  1. Upload your exported slideshow video or voiceover file to Google Drive.
  2. Open sharing settings and choose Anyone with the link.
  3. Make sure the permission says Viewer.
  4. Copy the link and paste it into your Canvas discussion reply.
  5. Open the link from Canvas to make sure the voiceover plays.
Post check: Your reply should include a greeting plus a Drive link classmates can view and hear.
Google Drive link note: If you share through Google Drive, make sure the file is viewable by anyone with the link, and make sure the permission is Viewer, not Editor or Commenter.
