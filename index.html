Step 1

Plan your two-minute video

Your video must be at least 1 minute and no longer than 2 minutes. Use the timing map and script builder below to draft what you want to say.

0:00 to 0:20Introduce your name and preferred pronouns.
0:20 to 0:50Share a little background, such as your hometown, program of study, interests, or goals.
0:50 to 1:25Share something meaningful or representative about you. This can be a story, object, hobby, photo, symbol, or creative work.
1:25 to 1:50Explain how ENGL& 101 might benefit you in your academic journey, career path, or personal life.
1:50 to 2:00End with a closing thought, thank you, or final detail you want classmates to remember.

Step 3

Post your video to Canvas

Use this walkthrough after you download or save your video. The studio page helps you create the work, but the final post still goes in the Canvas discussion board.

Canvas Upload Walkthrough

Post your video using the Canvas media/video option so classmates can watch it directly inside the discussion thread.

Canvas discussion reply
  1. Open the Canvas discussion and click Reply.
  2. Write a short greeting or context sentence.
  3. In the reply box toolbar, choose the media/video option, not the plain file attachment option.
  4. Upload or record your video through the media tool so it appears as a playable video inside your reply.
  5. Wait for the video to finish processing before posting.
  6. Post your reply, then play the video inside the discussion thread to make sure classmates can hear and see it.
Post check: Your reply should include a greeting plus a video player that can be watched directly in the discussion thread, not just a downloadable file attachment.
